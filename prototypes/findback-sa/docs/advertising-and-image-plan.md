# FindBack SA — advertising and imagery

9 October 2026. Advertising supports the intended free community service. This revision is still a prototype: no live advertiser enquiries, payments, ad-network scripts, impression collection or income.

## Implemented placements

- Labelled community sponsor panel beside public reports and hotspots on desktop.
- One labelled in-feed slot between sample reports, visible on mobile and desktop.
- A labelled area-sponsor banner on the hotspot view.
- Advertising information and a working sample enquiry preview. Enquiries are not transmitted or stored on a server.
- No advertisements within private sighting, report-submission or contact-preference forms. No sponsored ranking of vehicle reports.

## Suggested initial model

Start with direct monthly sponsorships from suitable local businesses. Possible categories include vehicle security, repair and roadside services. Review each advertiser and creative before publication; do not imply that payment establishes trust or endorsement. Establish rates after determining actual operating costs and available audience. Do not promise impressions, recoveries or revenue without supporting data.

Calculate the monthly funding target from hosting, storage, maintenance and moderation costs. An illustrative formula is: required sponsors = monthly funding target divided by net monthly fee per sponsor, rounded up. This is a budgeting method, not an earnings forecast. The fees and costs have not been set.

Later consider an ad network after the site is public, useful and eligible. The existing private demo cannot establish network approval or demand. Network activation needs the owner's approved publisher account, permitted placements, accurate privacy disclosures and an implementation-specific assessment of tracking/consent and data transfers. No publisher identifier or payment destination has been invented.

## Privacy boundaries

Do not sell or supply reporter contact details, precise sightings, stolen-vehicle identifiers or private message contents to advertisers. Community alerts must remain separate from marketing subscriptions. Direct marketing requires its own lawful processing assessment; do not assume consent to receive theft updates is consent to marketing. Prefer broad contextual placements over individual profiling. Actual compliance requires implementation and operating procedures, not just these interface labels.

## Generated image provenance

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Final originals:

Website copies are encoded as WebP under findback-sa/dist/assets. The three images are displayed with an AI illustration disclosure and can be enlarged through the report image viewer. They are not evidence or photos of stolen vehicles. Real public reports should use genuine reporter-supplied images; do not use AI output to fill missing evidence photos.

Final prompt set: landscape 3:2 photographic catalog images of (1) a white Hilux-like double-cab pickup with black canopy, (2) a silver Polo Vivo-like compact hatchback, and (3) a blue EcoSport-like compact SUV. All show the full vehicle from a front three-quarter angle in a neutral South African urban parking setting, restrained natural daylight, blank registrations, no identifiable people, readable logos, text, watermarks, police or crime-scene elements.
