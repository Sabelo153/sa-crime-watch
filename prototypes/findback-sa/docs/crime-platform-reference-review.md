# FindBack SA — reference review

9 October 2026. Curated set of 30 crime mapping, crime data and public safety services. This is not a global popularity ranking. Review used public pages, indexed first-party material and official guides; JavaScript-only dashboards, timeouts and access restrictions prevented full visual inspection of every interface. Design suggestions below are our interpretation, not claims that these sites endorse FindBack SA. No foreign incident data, logos, branding, or private records were copied.

## Applied design decisions

- Navy, slate and white establish a restrained information dashboard; small amber/red indicators communicate sample report volume or status.
- Direct labels replace motivational slogans and playful hearts/emoji.
- Community reporting remains the core surface; search and private sightings remain readily available.
- Vehicle descriptions use structured registration/date/contact fields and visible fictional/unverified provenance.
- Filtered record totals describe only the displayed sample records, not national crime totals.
- Hotspot categories, reporting periods, map legend and tabular zone counts are adjacent.
- Community and SAPS source explanations remain distinct. No claim of official affiliation, verified police records, live monitoring, predicted risk or legal certification.
- The map remains explicitly schematic. Its independent fictional illustration dataset is not derived from the three vehicle cards.
- Existing contact preferences, visibility controls, comments, sharing, recovery status and report management remain available.

## Still outside this prototype

Real accounts, server storage, live alerts/messages, active moderation, validated geographic boundaries, SAPS import, and implementation-specific South African legal review.
